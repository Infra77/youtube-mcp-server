# ADR-0009: bounded local swap for playlist selection

Status: accepted for local implementation; human review pending. Date: 2026-10-31.

Research basis: [[Language and Duration Metadata]], [[TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity]].

The original greedy regression selected a score-1 20-minute item instead of two 11-minute items
with scores .8/.7 under a 22-minute cap. That left two minutes unused and lower combined relevance.
Implement one deterministic one-for-two replacement pass, strictly improving combined score and
preserving all hard constraints and item-count bounds. Candidate cap is 50, with bounded O(n^3)
enumeration and no per-second dynamic-programming allocation. Equal gains preserve ranked iteration
order. The updated regression checks full 1320-second selection and input-order determinism.

Alternatives: full DP increases memory/time and requires a more explicit global objective; retain
plain greedy leaves the demonstrated defect. This local improvement is not a global optimum guarantee.
